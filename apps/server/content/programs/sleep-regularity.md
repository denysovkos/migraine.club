## Why try this

Irregular sleep schedules are associated with more frequent migraine attacks in observational studies. Keeping bed and wake times consistent — even on weekends — may reduce the trigger load.

## What we'll measure

- **Adherence**: percentage of days you logged bed and wake times within the target window.
- **Outcomes**: headache days during the 28 days before you started, compared with weeks 3 to 6 of the program.

## How it works

1. You pick a target bed time and wake time. Tolerance defaults to ±30 minutes.
2. Each day, log "On target" or "Off track" in the diary. If you log off-track, enter the actual times.
3. After 6 weeks, you'll see your adherence and a conservative outcome comparison.

## How to stop

You can pause or end the program at any time from the Programs settings.
